# Final entry

```
Late. One lamp still on in a row of dark windows. In one, a curtain drawn a hand's width aside, and the sky in the gap between cloth and glass. The whole sky. And behind it the stars, and behind the stars the same dark that holds the windows.
```

242 Unicode code points.

## Why this one

- **The line breaks are rests, not instructions.** `Late.` is a one-word stop; the middle runs long with two commas; `The whole sky.` is a full stop of three words placed exactly where the image widens; then one unbroken sentence rolls out to the stars and back. Short, long, short, long — the eye keeps being made to wait.
- **It shows rather than tells.** No one is told to look, breathe, or relax. The reader is simply set at a window with a lamp, a curtain pulled a hand's width, cloth, glass, sky, stars. Every word carries the picture or the measure; none can be cut without losing either.
- **Plain sounds do the slowing.** Soft liquids and sibilants (`still`, `sky`, `glass`, `stars`) and the repeated `and behind … and behind …` give the last sentence a low, even tread. Dropping `of them` from the old draft (`In one of them` → `In one`) removes a fussy referent and leaves a cleaner comma rest.
- **It dwarfs.** The gap is a hand wide; the whole sky fits in it; the stars sit behind that; and behind the stars is the same dark that held the windows at the beginning. Vast and small close into one dark, so the image keeps opening after the final stop.
- **Fresh.** No stock wellness language, no waves, no breath-counting, no "let go." The hand's-width gap and the dark shared by window and night are the text's own images.
